Photos for the deployment cards.

The images here are Weatherhaven product photos from weatherhavenusa.com. Each one shows the shelter system named in
that deployment's story (MECC, MEX-26, ATEPS, Endurance, Polarhaven) and is captioned "product photo", because they
are not photos of those specific sites. Real site photos are better: add them here and list them under `photos` in
globe.config.js by pin ID. That list overrides these defaults.
